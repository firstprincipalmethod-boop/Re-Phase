The software synchronizes 13 finite specifications (7 v53 and 6 v54), typed
computations, golden snapshots, generated JSON/CSV and reproducibility checks.
The fixed sources are Vol.5.3 corrected 2026-10-21 noheader-r1 (DOI 23499738)
and Vol.5.4 corrected 2026-10-21 (DOI 23499819). It reproduces the source gap,
equal-surviving-slices comparison and constant-velocity observation schedules;
separates sections from common-action policies; and distinguishes role metadata
from fifty computed finite predicates. Strict JSON/ID/domain checks and a
complete-record second implementation support the declared finite scope.

Current packaging removes internal revision suffixes and private execution
paths from public derivatives. The accompanying manuscript distinguishes old
preparation records from later review and uses readable, copyable ASCII CLI commands.
The thirteen computed objects and mathematical specifications are unchanged
from the reviewed r4, apart from edition identifiers and dependent digests.
