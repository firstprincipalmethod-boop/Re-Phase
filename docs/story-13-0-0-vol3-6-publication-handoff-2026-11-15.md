## Mathematical handoff

Vol.3.6 connects compatible candidates and a requested feature to the spectral structure of a known linear operator. It distinguishes full-state recovery, feature recovery, target guarantees, and invariant-kernel descent. Its development includes split semisimple and cyclic Fourier decompositions, primary/nilpotent structure, scalar extension, fixed observation menus, invariant dual subspaces, Gram/SVD noise bounds, and separately conditioned local/Gaussian and nonlinear invariant-representation connections.

Vol.3.3 supplies the operator-representation connection, while Vol.3.5 supplies the neighboring dual/inner-product viewpoint. The foundational role of Vol.3.6 for already published Vols.4.5–4.6 concerns theoretical exposition, not publication chronology; Vol.4.4 supplies the neighboring local-directional viewpoint.

For future integration, retain the original candidate space, admissible subset, observation, requested feature, field, and all hypotheses for each modal or reduced representation. A recoverable feature and an updatable reduced dynamics are separate requirements. Exact recovery and quantitative stability have separate assumptions.
